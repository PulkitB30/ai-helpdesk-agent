# Wi-Fi

## Networks
| Network | For | Password |
|---|---|---|
| `Company-Staff` | Company laptops and phones | Automatic (company certificate) |
| `Company-Guest` | Visitors and private devices | Changes every Monday, shown at reception |

Private devices must **never** use `Company-Staff`.

## Problems
- **Laptop does not connect to Company-Staff**: restart Wi-Fi. If it still fails, your certificate may be expired - create a ticket with category `network`.
- **Weak signal in the warehouse**: use the access point near gate 2, or a LAN cable at the warehouse office.
- **Guest password does not work**: it changed on Monday; ask reception for the new one.

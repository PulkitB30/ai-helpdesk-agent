# Passwords and Account Lockout

## Password rules
- At least **12 characters**.
- Must contain upper case, lower case and a number.
- You cannot reuse your last **5 passwords**.
- Passwords expire every **180 days**. You get an email reminder 14 days before.

## Account lockout
After **5 wrong password attempts** your account is locked for **15 minutes**.
It unlocks automatically after 15 minutes. If you need access sooner, ask the IT helpdesk for a password reset.

## Reset your password
- Use the helpdesk assistant ("reset my password") - you get a reset link by email.
- Or go to `https://passwordreset.example.com` from any browser.
- Disabled accounts (for example after leaving the company or long absence) cannot be reset by yourself. Create a ticket with category `account`.

## Never
- Never share your password, not even with IT. IT will never ask for it.
- Never write it on a sticky note at your desk.

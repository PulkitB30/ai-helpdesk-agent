# Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

All employees must use MFA for email, VPN and Microsoft 365.

## Setup
1. Install **Microsoft Authenticator** on your phone (App Store / Google Play).
2. On your computer go to `https://aka.ms/mfasetup` and log in.
3. Choose "Mobile app" and scan the QR code with Authenticator.
4. Approve the test notification.

## New phone
Before you reset or give away your old phone, move your accounts:
open Authenticator > Settings > Backup. If the old phone is already gone,
create a ticket with category `account` - IT must reset your MFA. This takes up to 1 working day.

## Problems
- **No notification arrives**: open the Authenticator app manually, pull down to refresh.
- **Wrong time on phone**: codes only work if the phone time is set to automatic.

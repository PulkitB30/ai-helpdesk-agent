# Printers

## Office printers
| Name | Location | Type |
|---|---|---|
| PRN-OFFICE-1 | 1st floor, next to kitchen | Colour, A4/A3 |
| PRN-WAREHOUSE | Warehouse office | Black and white, A4 |

## Add a printer
- Windows: Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers > Add device. Choose `PRN-OFFICE-1`.
- Mac: System Settings > Printers & Scanners > Add (+).
You must be in the office network or connected to VPN.

## Common problems
- **Paper jam**: open front door A, remove paper carefully. Never pull torn paper with force.
- **Print job stuck**: open the print queue, cancel all jobs, restart the printer (power button 10 seconds).
- **Toner low**: spare toner is in the cabinet next to PRN-OFFICE-1. Tell IT when you take the last one.
- **"Printer offline"**: check that you are in the office network or on VPN.

For hardware damage create a ticket with category `hardware`.

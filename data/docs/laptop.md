# Laptops

## Standard equipment
Every office employee gets a laptop (Windows 11 or MacBook), a docking station and a headset.
Warehouse staff share the PCs in the warehouse office.

## Rules
- Lock your screen when you leave your desk: Windows+L or Ctrl+Cmd+Q on Mac.
- Restart at least once a week so updates are installed.
- Disk encryption (BitLocker / FileVault) must stay on.

## Problems
- **Laptop is slow**: restart first. Check that at least 10 % disk space is free.
- **Screen flickers or broken hardware**: create a ticket with category `hardware`. You can get a loaner laptop from IT within 1 working day.
- **Lost or stolen laptop**: tell IT immediately (priority `high`) so we can lock it remotely. Also report theft to the police.

## Leaving the company
Return laptop, charger and badge to IT on your last day.
